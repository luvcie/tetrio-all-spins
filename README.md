# Learning All-Spins in TETR.IO

TKI helped me find TSDs midgame, and C-Spin and DT Cannon helped me find TSTs. I’m looking for openers that do the same for S/Z, L/J, and I spins.

The goal is to get better at building the cavities midgame and have more ways to keep back-to-back (B2B) going.

## Small glossary

- TD - T-Spin Triple + T-Spin Double
- SS - Spin Single
- B2B - Back to back. Spin clears, including minis, and Quads extend the streak. Placements without a clear leave it alone. A regular Single, Double, or Triple breaks it.

In Tetra League, a Mini Spin Single has 0 base attack and normally adds 1 line if it extends B2B. Attack cancels pending garbage first, then sends any leftover. Breaking B2B releases the stored Surge.

| Mini Spin Single takes B2B from | Immediate attack (normally) | Stored Surge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ×2 to ×3 | 1 line | 0 lines |
| ×3 to ×4 | 1 line | Starts at 4 lines |
| ×7 to ×8 | 1 line | Adds 1 line, bringing the total to 8 |
